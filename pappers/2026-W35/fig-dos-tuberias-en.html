Two pipes that never cross

BDC’s premium content exists under a condition that never makes the cover slides: your ERP has to be in SAP’s cloud.

Premium content
SAP-managed data products
Requirement: ERP in SAP’s cloud. On-premise systems don’t even appear in the formation (KBA 3786663).
S/4HANA private cloudS/4HANA public cloudSuccessFactors
259 standard data products in early 2026, mostly master data. They land in the Foundation Services object store.
Everything else
Your own data products
They come in through Datasphere connectivity. It works, but the managed catalog does not apply.
ECCS/4 on-premiseZ fieldsCDS viewsOracleSQL ServerSalesforceSnowflakeBigQuery
They land in the Datasphere object store, which is a different one. Managed products don’t pass through here; yours don’t pass through there.
§3.3
Your Databricks may receive a data product and hold it copied temporarily and only so queries run faster. What it may not do is pass it along: Power BI, Tableau, an external lake, another tenant.
Your lakehouse stops being the layer everything is served from and becomes an authorized consumer, not a redistributor.
Sources: SAP, Business Data Cloud — FAQs (official blog); SAP KBA 3786663; SAP BDC Supplemental Terms and Conditions v7-2026, §3.3 and §1.11; public data product catalog in the SAP Discovery Center. The 259 count: Expertum, January 2026.
