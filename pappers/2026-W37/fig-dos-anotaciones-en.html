Two annotations, four destinations

For a CDS view to be extractable you need two declarations, not one. And the family of the data does not live in a single annotation: it lives in two.

Mandatory
@Analytics.dataExtraction.enabled
Without it the view exists and can be reported on, but it is invisible to extraction.
Annotation 1
@Analytics.dataCategory
#DIMENSION
Master data · attributes
ODP $P
#FACT · #CUBE
Transactional · facts
ODP $F
Annotation 2
@ObjectModel.dataCategory
#TEXT
Texts
ODP $T
#HIERARCHY
Hierarchies
ODP $H
With no dataCategory the suffix is $E: the view is extractable but unclassified, and a generic object is born on the other side. And #MASTER_DATA does not exist — an attributes view carries no @ObjectModel.dataCategory at all.
Declaring the wrong family does not produce an error: it produces the wrong container, which you then have to delete and rebuild. The ODP name suffix is the five-second check.
Source: SAP Learning, Working with ODP Context: CDS view based extraction. The formal condition is a dataCategory —from either annotation— and dataExtraction.enabled.
