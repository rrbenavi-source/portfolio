Where young employment falls, and why it matters

Substitutes
Codified knowledge
The formal, standardized kind: written down, and teachable through a manual, a course, or a documented procedure.
−19%
Employment among 22-to-25-year-olds in the most exposed occupations, against their less exposed peers.
Complements
Tacit knowledge
The kind acquired only through practice, mentorship, and repeated exposure to real situations. It isn't written down anywhere.
Grows
Employment among experienced workers in the occupations that rely on it.
The management consequence
Tacit knowledge isn't taught. It gets manufactured by passing through the codified kind.
The mechanical work AI absorbed was boring, slow, and worth little to the business. It was also the only curriculum we had.
Source: Brynjolfsson, Chandar and Chen, Canaries in the Coal Mine?, Stanford Digital Economy Lab, August 2026 revision (ADP payroll data through June 2026). The authors present this as descriptive rather than causal evidence.
