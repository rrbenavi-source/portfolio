The eight annotations that actually matter

There are hundreds of CDS annotations. For reporting in SAC, these are the ones that simply cannot be missing, and what breaks when they are.

Annotation · where it goes
What happens if it is missing
1
@Analytics.dataCategory: #DIMENSION · #CUBE
Master data as dimension, facts as cube.
The engine does not know what is the star and what is the center.
2
@Analytics.query: true
Only on the consumption layer.
The view does not appear in the SAC live connection.
3
@ObjectModel.representativeKey
On every dimension.
With several keys, the engine picks the wrong one.
4
@Semantics.text: true · @ObjectModel.text.association
Description in the same view or in another one.
SAC shows you nothing but codes.
5
@Semantics.amount.currencyCode · @Semantics.quantity.unitOfMeasure
On every amount and quantity, with its partner field.
Pesos get added to dollars, pieces to tons.
6
@DefaultAggregation: #SUM · #MAX · #NONE
On every measure.
Every figure inherits a behavior nobody chose.
7
@AnalyticsDetails.query.axis · .display · .totals · .formula
On the query. What used to be configured in BEx.
Layout, texts and formulas get rebuilt in every story.
8
@AccessControl.authorizationCheck: #CHECK + DCL
View by view. The engine applies it on every request.
Everyone sees everything. In BW the analysis authorization did this.
The ninth, @Analytics.dataExtraction.enabled, is not for SAC: it is the ODP replication door into a warehouse or a lake. Another door, another contract.
Sources: SAP Help, Analytics Annotations and AnalyticsDetails Annotations (SAP NetWeaver 7.5) · SAP Help, Live Data Connections to SAP S/4HANA.
