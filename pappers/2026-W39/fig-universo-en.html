The hidden universe

The semantic layer does not disappear when you switch off the tool that held it. It only moves. And there are just two places it can land.

It used to live in

The BO universe and the BEx query
  • Report filters nobody had written down
  • Rounding rules
  • Business mappings and labels
  • Hierarchies and derived attributes
  • Historical data BW loaded at the time
→
The CDS view · once
Annotated in the cube and the query. Every story consumes it the same way. Tested in RSRT.
Every story · every time
By hand, in the front end, in triplicate. And at some point the versions diverge.

What showed up when reconciling BO against SAC

Unidentified filters
Years that matched and years that did not; SAC brought back more than BO did.
Rounding
BO rounded to whole numbers; SAC showed the decimals. Same number, looked like two.
The historical year
Recent years matched; the oldest did not. A warehouse keeps the photo; an ERP keeps the state.
Logic in the story
"If the planning point is 01, then North." A business mapping written in the front end.
Whatever you do not annotate in layer 2 or layer 3, you will find rewritten in the front end, right when it matters, in triplicate.
