A checkmark is not a reconciliation

The badge each platform offers, what it really certifies, and what none of them does: compare today's number against its source.

Tool
What it certifies
What it doesn't do
Power BI / Fabric
Certified
That an authorized reviewer attested it meets the organization's standards.
If the model changes tomorrow, the badge stays where it was.
Databricks Unity Catalog
certification_status
That the asset met internal standards. By hand or via rules on usage, owner or age.
No rule checks whether the number reconciles.
Genie
benchmarks
Up to 500 questions with their reference SQL; grades good, bad or manual review.
Runs when someone launches it. If the reference SQL is wrong, it grades against another error.
Snowflake
Data Metric Functions
Nulls, duplicates, freshness, on their own schedule: hourly by default.
Measures data quality, not whether the KPI ties to the books.
SAP Datasphere
catalog · glossary
That the KPI has a written definition and the asset is published.
A good definition doesn't prove today's number meets it.
The witness
control totals
Compares the KPI against the ledger after every load, transport and upgrade.
Says whether it reconciles today, and shows it on the dashboard.
A badge says who thought the data was trustworthy. A reconciliation proves it is today.
Sources: Microsoft Learn, Endorse Fabric and Power BI items · Databricks, Flag data as certified or deprecated and Genie benchmarks · Snowflake, Introduction to data quality checks · SAP Help, SAP Datasphere catalog.
