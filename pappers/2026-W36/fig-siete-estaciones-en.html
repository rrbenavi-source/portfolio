Seven stops, seven tests

A sales query's journey from BW to Business Data Cloud, and what to demand at every leg of it.

01
The lift
ValidateThat your release qualifies, that cleanup happened first, and that the link holds: extraction from your ERP now crosses the network.
02
The query becomes an analytic model
ValidateNot that the object exists: that the number matches cell by cell against the original query, same variables, same day.
03
Where the number now lives
ValidateResponse times at production volume, currency conversion, fiscal year variant, texts per language, and non-cumulative key figures, which must be rebuilt.
04
Where the report is consumed
ValidateAnalysis for Office cannot read analytic models. Your Excel controllers move back into the SAC family, and that means capacity units per user.
05
Authorizations
ValidateWildcards don't exist in Data Access Controls. Log in as a regional manager and confirm they don't see a single extra row.
06
BPC and your Z developments
AskIn writing: are your modifications around BPC still admissible inside BW running in SAP's cloud, and under which category?
07
And only then, the data for AI
LaterThe Data Product Generator and delta share into your lakehouse come last, and under the contractual boundary from the previous issue.
The number that reaches the dashboard after the move has to be the same one that reached it before. Proving that is work, not an assumption.
Sources: SAP note 2932647 v18; SAP Learning, Query Template Generator and Introducing Model Transfer; KBA 3297935; SAP BDC Supplemental Terms and Conditions v7-2026.
