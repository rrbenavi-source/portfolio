The ODP-RFC clock

The service entrance everyone used to pull data out of SAP closed in four moves. The first three already happened, this year.

9 June 2026
It stops being permitted, and stops being possible
Note 3255746 reaches version 12: use of ODP-RFC by the customer or by third parties against ABAP systems is prohibited. The same day, security note 3748819 delivers the mechanism: it validates every incoming call and blocks the unpermitted ones.
27 July 2026
An escape valve, at your own risk
Note 3731818 ships the report RODPS_REPL_SECUREACCESS_OPTOUT, which suspends the block. SAP describes it as strictly temporary and applied at the customer’s own risk.
9 September 2026
The tool to find out who is touching the door
Note 3439624, version 34, installs the self-assessment. It only assesses calls from the day you install it: it does not look backwards.
31 December 2026
The exception runs out
The temporary valve expires. SAP has already written that it then reinstates the protections and that they are «enforced without exception».
If you have old integrations pointing at that interface, they will not degrade. They will stop.
Sources: SAP Notes 3255746 (v12), 3748819, 3731818 (v4) and 3439624 (v34), consulted in SAP for Me.
