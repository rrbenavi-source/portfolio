The data’s journey

From the S/4HANA table to the report in Fabric there are five stops. At the second one, the data proves it is allowed to leave and pays its duty.

SAP
S/4HANA
CDS view enabled for extraction
Carries the ERP’s business context. Needs @Analytics.dataExtraction.enabled and must be C1-released.
↓
SAP
Datasphere
Replication flow · the customs house
Reads from the CDS_EXTRACTION container and writes outward. Initial load and delta.
Toll · Premium Outbound Integration
↓  this is where it is charged, by the gigabyte, every month
Azure
ADLS Gen2
Parquet files in a container
Format required by the destination: Parquet, with Group Delta = None.
↓
Microsoft
OneLake
Shortcut to the container
Access to the storage without duplicating it again.
↓
Microsoft
Fabric
Mirrored database
Delta tables and a SQL analytics endpoint. From here on, it is no longer SAP.
They left SAP analytics. The data, in order to leave, still goes through an SAP product.
Sources: Microsoft Learn, Mirrored Databases From SAP and the mirroring-via-SAP-Datasphere tutorial · SAP Datasphere documentation, Premium Outbound Integration.
