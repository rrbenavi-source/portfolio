Four doors and their toll

Since June 2026, moving data out of an ABAP system toward a non-SAP platform has few legitimate routes. Each one’s toll is different — and two of them charge every month.

The door
What it costs
1
SAP Datasphere · Premium Outbound Integration
A replication flow reads the CDS views enabled for extraction and writes to external storage.
The door in this case
Blocks of 20 GB of outbound volume per month.
Recurring · no shut-off
2
The ODP OData API
The same ODP layer, through its REST interface instead of RFC. One of the two alternatives the note names.
No volume toll. The ceiling is throughput.
No cost per GB
3
Certified partner connector · Open Mirroring
Native Fabric APIs. Five SAP-certified solutions: DAB, ASAPIO, Theobald, Simplement, SNP Glue.
Third-party software licence.
Recurring · fixed amount
4
SAP Business Data Cloud
The other alternative the note names. Zero-copy architecture: sharing data without duplicating it.
Subscription to a new SAP platform.
Recurring · platform
Note 3255746 does not mention Datasphere among the alternatives. That is not an oversight: Datasphere is an SAP application, so its traffic is permitted by definition. The prohibition was never about the protocol.
Sources: SAP Note 3255746 v12 (9 June 2026) · SAP Datasphere documentation, Premium Outbound Integration · Microsoft Learn, Extract SAP data to Microsoft Fabric.
